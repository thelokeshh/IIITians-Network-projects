# IIITians Network

### A Unified Student Ecosystem Connecting IIITs Across India

[Website](https://www.iiitiansnetwork.in/) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lokesh-meena-029244288/)

---

## 📌 Overview

**IIITians Network** is a student-led platform built to connect students, alumni, student clubs, opportunities, events, and campus information across IIITs in India.

The platform aims to reduce the fragmentation of information that exists across individual college websites, Instagram pages, WhatsApp groups, Telegram communities, placement reports, and alumni networks by bringing important student resources into a unified ecosystem.

As President of IIITians Network, I led the product direction, requirements, prioritization, cross-functional execution, validation, launch, and adoption of the platform.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Students across IIITs often rely on multiple disconnected sources to find:

- Inter-IIIT events and competitions
- Internship and job opportunities
- Placement information
- Alumni connections
- Student clubs and organizations
- Campus information and experiences
- College and branch information
- Relevant student communities

Similarly, student clubs and event organizers face challenges in reaching students beyond their own campus.

This created an opportunity to build a centralized, student-first IIIT ecosystem.

---

## 💡 Product Vision

> **Build a unified digital ecosystem that connects IIIT students, alumni, clubs, opportunities and events across India.**

The platform was designed around four key principles:

- **Centralization** — bring fragmented student resources together
- **Accessibility** — make information easier to discover
- **Community** — strengthen connections between IIIT students and alumni
- **Collaboration** — enable clubs, organizations and external partners to reach the wider IIIT ecosystem

## 🧩 Product Ecosystem

The platform is organized into multiple interconnected modules.

### Events Desk
Centralized discovery of events, fests, competitions and hackathons across IIITs.

### Opportunities
A dedicated space for:

- Internships
- Full-time roles
- Research
- Open Source
- Hackathons
- Startup opportunities

### IIIT Directory
Searchable information across IIITs, including:

- College information
- Location
- Placement information
- Campus details
- Branch information

### Network Legacy
A structured alumni network connecting previous and current batches.

### Clubs & Organizations
Enables student organizations to register and publish announcements and events.

### Gallery
Students can contribute campus photographs and experiences for the wider community.

### Collaboration
A structured collaboration channel for:

- Startups
- Communities
- Student Clubs
- Companies

### Merchandise
A centralized merchandise experience for the Network community.

## 🔍 Product Discovery & User Research

Before development, the team identified recurring problems through discussions with students, alumni, aspirants and student organizations.

Feedback was collected through:

- WhatsApp
- Discord
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- Direct conversations
- Alumni interactions
- Internal team discussions

Approximately **100 users and stakeholders** were engaged during the discovery and feedback process.

Key recurring needs included:

- A common place for IIIT events
- Reliable and accessible placement information
- Better alumni discoverability
- Easier club/event promotion
- Centralized opportunities
- Better cross-IIIT student interaction

These insights were translated into product requirements and feature priorities.
